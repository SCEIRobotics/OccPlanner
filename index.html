03 / L3ROcc

Local 3D Reconstruction with Occupancy

L3ROcc converts monocular RGB video sequences into temporally consistent, robot-centric supervision: sparse visible occupancy, binary visibility masks, and aligned camera trajectories. It reconstructs shared scene geometry and camera motion with π³, then aligns geometry, poses, and viewing rays to the robot base frame for visibility-aware occupancy generation.

01

Reconstruct

Recover shared scene geometry, local pointmaps, confidence maps, sparse camera poses, and viewing rays with π³, then densify the camera trajectory.

02

Align

Estimate an approximately metric scene scale when reference poses are available, then transform geometry, poses, and viewing rays into the robot base frame.

03

Label

Voxelize aligned local geometry into candidate occupancy. Ray marching labels space before the first hit as free, the first hit as occupied, and space behind it as unobserved.

Training only. L3ROcc outputs sparse visible-occupancy sequences, packed binary visibility masks, and aligned camera trajectory metadata. It is not required during OccPlanner inference.

04 / OccPlanner

Goal-Aware Occupancy-Conditioned Diffusion Planner

OccPlanner is built on a shared π³-based RGB-D geometric encoder. It predicts voxel occupancy, distills near-ground occupancy features into compact tokens, and progressively integrates pixel-goal, temporal-context, and occupancy cues before denoising future actions into a trajectory.

Training objectives. OccPlanner combines a focal voxel-occupancy loss, a Smooth L1 diffusion-noise loss, and an auxiliary ego-goal regression loss. PointGoal supervision is used only during training.

05 / Results

Obstacle-aware trajectories in simulation and the real world.

We evaluate closed-loop navigation on the same fixed set of 6,000 episodes across 60 unseen indoor scenes, covering 3–5 m and 5–8 m start–goal ranges and reporting SR, SPL, and DTG. We then evaluate zero-shot sim-to-real transfer and real-world adaptation on RGB-D sequences recorded by a Unitree G1.

Simulation

Consistent pixel-goal grounding across diverse scene layouts.

Across home, commercial, cluttered-easy, and cluttered-hard scenes, OccPlanner predicts smooth trajectories that remain within inferred traversable regions while avoiding observed obstacles. Its larger gains under long-range and cluttered settings indicate improved robustness to extended planning horizons and complex local geometry.

Real world

Zero-shot transfer and real-world adaptation.

We evaluate OccPlanner in an open-loop setting using RGB-D sequences recorded by a Unitree G1 in a cluttered office. The simulator-trained model captures coarse obstacle structure and produces goal-conditioned trajectories without real-world fine-tuning; fine-tuning on a disjoint set of real-world sequences yields denser, more spatially coherent occupancy while preserving smooth trajectories through inferred free space.

Evaluation boundary

These qualitative results evaluate occupancy and trajectory prediction on recorded real-world RGB-D sequences in an open-loop setting. They provide initial evidence of sim-to-real transfer and adaptation, but do not claim physical closed-loop deployment.
